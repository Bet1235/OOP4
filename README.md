## TITLE: POS WITHDRAWAL SYSTEM
### DESCRIPTION: this system is a machine that takes a customer's card, gets the account information and sends a message to the bank requesting a withdrawal in real-time. After the withdrawal is made, the credit amount is sent to the bank account of the owner POS machine. Cash is then given to the customer by the owner of the machine. It saves time from standing in the queue at banks and for emergency spending. All bank Account holder's can use th machine.
